# Privacy Policy — AI Prompt Enhancer

_Last updated: April 26, 2026_

## Overview

AI Prompt Enhancer is a Chrome extension that improves text prompts locally on your device. This policy explains what data the extension does and does not collect.

## Data Collection

**We collect no user data.**

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information, browsing history, prompt content, or any other user data.

## How the Extension Works

When you click the Enhance button, your text is sent exclusively to a locally running instance of [Ollama](https://ollama.com) on your own machine (`127.0.0.1:11434`). The request never leaves your device. No third-party servers, analytics services, or external APIs are contacted at any point.

## Local Storage

The extension uses `chrome.storage.local` solely to save your preferences:
- Whether the extension is globally enabled or disabled
- A list of domains where you have disabled the extension
- A flag indicating whether the one-time setup wizard has been completed

None of this information is transmitted anywhere.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.
